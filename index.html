Frequently Asked Questions

What is Science?
Science is the study of the natural world and the laws that govern it.
What is Technology?
Technology refers to the tools, machines, and systems used to solve problems and improve our lives.
What is Mathematics?
Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, and shapes.
What is Engineering?
Engineering is the use of scientific principles to design and build machines, structures, and other items.
